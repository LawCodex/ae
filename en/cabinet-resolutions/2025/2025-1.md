# Cabinet Resolution No. (1) of 2025 Regarding Approving the List of Terrorist Persons and Organizations

## Article (1)

Both lists of terrorist persons and terrorist organizations attached hereto are hereby approved.

## Article (2)

This Resolution shall be published in the Official Gazette and shall enter into force as of the date of its issuance.
