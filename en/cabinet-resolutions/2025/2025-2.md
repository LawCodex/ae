# Cabinet Resolution No. (2) of 2025 Regarding the Technical Regulation for Tobacco-Free Nicotine Pouches

## Article (1)

The Technical Regulations for Tobacco-Free Nicotine Pouches, the number and title of which are shown in the Table attached to this Resolution, shall be deemed mandatory technical regulations in the United Arab Emirates.

## Article (2)

This Resolution shall be published in the Official Gazette, and shall enter into force one hundred eighty (180) days following the date of its publication.
