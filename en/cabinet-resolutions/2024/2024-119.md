# Cabinet Resolution No. (119) of 2024 Regarding Approving the Integrated Customs Tariff Nomenclature According to the Harmonized Commodity Description and Coding System of the GCC States (2022) and its Updates

## Article (1)

The Integrated Customs Tariff Nomenclature According to the Harmonized Commodity Description and Coding System of the GCC States (2022) and its Updates, attached hereto, shall be approved.

## Article (2)

Cabinet Resolution No. (17) of 2022 Approving the Unified Commodity Description and Coding Nomenclature of the GCC States According to the (2022) Harmonized System, and Updates thereto shall be repealed, as well as any provision contravening or contradicting this Resolution.

## Article (3)

This Resolution shall be published in the Official Gazette and shall enter into force as of 01 January 2025.
