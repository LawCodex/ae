# Cabinet Resolution No. (123) of 2024 On the Approval of the Lists of Prohibited and Restricted Goods in the States of the GCC

## Article (1)

Lists of Prohibited and Restricted Goods in the States of the GCC approved by Financial and Economic Cooperation Committee of the Gulf Cooperation Council attached to this Resolution shall be approved.

## Article (2)

The competent entities in the State shall adhere to the lists of prohibited and restricted goods, each within its jurisdiction. The Federal Authority for Identity, Citizenship, Customs, and Port Security shall be responsible for ensuring the enforcement of these lists of prohibited and restricted goods across the State.

## Article (3)

Any provision that violates or contradicts the provisions of this Resolution shall be repealed.

## Article (4)

This Resolution shall be published in the Official Gazette and shall enter into force as of 01 January 2025.
