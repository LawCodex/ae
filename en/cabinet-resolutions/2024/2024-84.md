# Cabinet Resolution No. (84) of 2024 Concerning Technical Requirements for Emergency Call (eCall) in Vehicles

## Article (1)

The Technical Requirements Regulation for eCall in Vehicles (UAE.S 5019: 2024) shall be a mandatory technical regulation in the United Arab Emirates.

## Article (2)

Standard No. (UAE.S 5019: 2018), approved by Cabinet Resolution No. (13) of 2018, shall be repealed.

## Article (3)

This Resolution shall be published in the Official Gazette and enter into force as of the day following the date of its publication.
