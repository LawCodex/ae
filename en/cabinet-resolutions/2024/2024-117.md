# Cabinet Resolution No. (117) of 2024 On the Technical Regulation for Safety Requirements for Modified Vehicles

## Article (1)

The Technical Regulation for Safety Requirements for Modified cars, the number and title of which are shown in the Table attached to this Resolution, is a Technical Regulation that is mandatory to be applied in the United Arab Emirates.

## Article (2)

The Technical Regulation No. (UAE.S 5014:2016) with the title Safety Requirements for Modified Vehicles approved by the aforementioned Cabinet Resolution No. (45) of 2016 shall be repealed.

## Article (3)

This Resolution shall be published in the Official Gazette and shall enter into force one hundred and eighty (180) days after the date of its publication.
