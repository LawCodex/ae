2. All coordinates shown in Table (A-B) of this article are determined on the World Geodetic System Geographic-WGS-84.

3. The Straight Baselines shown in Table (A-B) of this Article shall be used to demarcate UAE Maritime Areas.

## Article (2)

The waters located on the landward side of the Straight Baselines stipulated in Article One of this Resolution shall be considered internal waters of UAE in accordance with the provisions of Federal Law No. (19) of 1993 regarding the designation of UAE Maritime Zones.

## Article (3)

Cabinet Resolution No. (5) of 2009 concerning the application of the Straight Baseline System on UAE Coasts shall be repealed.

## Article (4)

This Resolution shall be published in the Official Gazette and shall enter into force as of the day following the date of its publication.
