# Cabinet Resolution No. (121) of 2022 Regarding the Approval of the Articles of Association of Emirates Post Group Company (Public Joint Stock Company)

## Article (1)

The Articles of Association of Emirates Post Group Company (Public Joint Stock Company), attached hereto, are hereby approved.

## Article (2)

This Resolution shall be published in the Official Gazette and shall enter into force on the day following the date of its issuance.
