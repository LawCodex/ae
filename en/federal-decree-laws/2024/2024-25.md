# Federal Decree by Law No. (25) of 2024 Concerning the Practice of Economic Activities in the State by Citizens of GCC States

## Article (1)

Citizens of States of the Gulf Cooperative Council (GCC) – whether natural or legal persons – shall be treated the same way citizens of the State are when practicing economic activities and professions in the State. With the exception of economic activities and professions designated by a resolution from the GCC Supreme Council or which are excluded, or certain conditions are required for its practice pursuant to a Cabinet resolution.

## Article (2)

Federal Law No. (2) of 1984 Concerning the Practice of Economic Activities in the State by Citizens of the GCC States shall be repealed.

## Article (3)

This Decree by Law shall be published in the Official Gazette and shall come into force as of the day following its date of publication.
